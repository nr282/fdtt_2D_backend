A key goal of the disaggregation software is to accomplish the following:
    1. depixelation
    2. decompression
    3. disaggregation of a 2D domain


These techniques can be applied to:
    1. Partial Differential Equations
    2. Images
        1. De-pixelation
        2. Decompression
        3. Disaggregation
